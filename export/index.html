Why BWP plywood for the Gulf

Kitchens and bathrooms in the Gulf see heat, humidity and daily washing, and coastal cities stay humid for much of the year. A waterproof phenolic bond (BWP, IS 710) keeps the layers from separating. MR plywood is the economical choice for furniture in dry, air-conditioned rooms.
