What calibrated plywood is

Calibrated plywood is sanded after pressing so the thickness is the same across the whole sheet. When every panel is the same thickness, joints line up, edge banding sits flush and laminates stay flat. That matters most for machine-cut modular furniture.

Where to use calibrated plywood

  • Modular kitchens and wardrobes
  • Laminate and veneer finishes
  • CNC and panel-saw cutting
  • Any work where panels must line up exactly

Calibrated and water resistance

Calibration is about thickness, not glue. For kitchens and wet areas, the bond matters more: ask us about BWP grade.

Questions about Calibrated Plywood

What does calibrated plywood mean?

It is sanded after pressing so the thickness is even across the whole sheet.

Why use calibrated plywood for modular furniture?

Even thickness gives tight joints, flush edge banding and flat laminate work, especially on machine-cut panels.

Is calibrated plywood waterproof?

Calibration is about thickness, not the bond. For wet areas, the bond is what counts, so ask about BWP grade.
