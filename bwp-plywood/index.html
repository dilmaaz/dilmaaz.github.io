What BWP plywood is

BWP stands for boiling water proof. Its layers are bonded with a waterproof phenolic resin, so the plywood holds together through steam, splashes and humidity that would make ordinary plywood delaminate. PlyPal's BWP plywood will be made to IS 710, the Indian Standard for marine plywood.

Where to use BWP plywood

  • Kitchen cabinets: carcasses, shutters and shelves
  • Bathroom vanities and utility areas
  • Furniture in humid, coastal homes
  • Any joinery that may get wet

Why it matters on the coast

In Dakshina Kannada and Udupi the air stays humid for much of the year. Lower-grade plywood can soften and swell in those conditions. A waterproof bond keeps kitchen and bathroom furniture solid for longer.

Choosing a thickness

  • 18 mm: the usual choice for kitchen carcasses and shutters
  • 12 mm: shelves and partitions
  • 6–9 mm: backs and panels

Questions about BWP Plywood

Is BWP plywood the same as marine plywood?

IS 710 is the Indian Standard for marine plywood, and it uses a boiling-water-proof bond. PlyPal's BWP plywood will be made to IS 710.

Which thickness is best for kitchen cabinets?

18 mm is the usual thickness for kitchen carcasses and shutters. 12 mm works for shelves.

Is BWP plywood worth it for bedrooms?

Usually not. Bedrooms stay dry, so MR plywood (IS 303) does the job for less money.
