What MR plywood is

MR stands for moisture resistant. MR grade plywood, often called plyboard, is general-purpose plywood made to IS 303, the Indian Standard for plywood. Its glue line handles normal indoor humidity, which makes it the economical choice for furniture and interiors that stay dry.

Where to use MR plywood

  • Wardrobes, beds and storage units
  • Bedroom and living-room furniture
  • Partitions and wall panelling
  • False ceilings and backing panels (thinner sheets)

Where not to use it

Don't use MR plywood anywhere that gets wet: kitchens, bathroom vanities, or areas near water. Use BWP plywood (IS 710) there instead.

Choosing a thickness

  • 6–9 mm: panelling, ceilings, drawer bases and backs
  • 12 mm: partitions and shelves
  • 16–18 mm: furniture frames, carcasses and shutters
  • 25 mm: heavy-duty tops

Questions about MR Plywood

Is MR plywood waterproof?

No. MR plywood resists normal indoor humidity but is not waterproof. For kitchens, bathrooms and wet areas use BWP plywood (IS 710).

What is MR plywood used for?

Wardrobes, beds, storage units, partitions, wall panelling and other furniture in dry rooms.
