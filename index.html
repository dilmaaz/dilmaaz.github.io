How it's made

From log to finished sheet

Every stage will happen under one roof at our Belthangady factory, so we can check quality at each step. Here's how a sheet is made.

01
Peeling

STEP 01

Peeling

Logs are debarked and peeled into thin, even veneers.

STEP 02

Drying

Veneers are dried to the right moisture level so they bond well.

STEP 03

Glue spreading

Resin goes on evenly and each layer is laid across the grain.

STEP 04

Hot pressing

The layers are pressed together under heat into one solid sheet.

STEP 05

Trimming

The sheets are trimmed square to a true 8 × 4 ft.

STEP 06

Sanding & testing

Faces are sanded and every batch checked in the factory lab.

What plywood does PlyPal make?

PlyPal will make MR grade plywood to IS 303, BWP plywood to IS 710, blockboard to IS 1659 and calibrated plywood, all in standard 8 × 4 ft sheets.

What is the difference between MR and BWP plywood?

MR (moisture resistant) plywood is bonded for dry indoor use such as wardrobes, beds and wall panelling. BWP (boiling water proof) plywood is bonded with a waterproof phenolic resin, so it suits kitchens, bathroom vanities and humid coastal homes.

Which plywood should I use for a kitchen?

Use BWP plywood (IS 710). Kitchens get steam, spills and washing every day, and BWP's waterproof bond keeps the layers from separating. 18 mm is the usual thickness for carcasses and shutters.
